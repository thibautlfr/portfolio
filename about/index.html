I'm Thibaut, a full-stack developer based in France with a strong pull toward the creative side of the web. I like building things that feel as good as they work, whether that means crafting smooth animations, thinking through an architecture, or getting the details right on a UI.

I studied computer science at IUT La Rochelle, where I completed a three-year BUT Informatique with a full-stack specialization. During that time, I joined Raccourci Agency (opens in new tab) as an apprentice, first on back-end work and then across the full stack, contributing to architecture decisions, mentoring, code reviews, and sprint planning alongside development.

In 2026, after completing a master's-level program at OpenClassrooms to earn the RNCP level 7 title in software development, I started freelancing under my own structure, Arko. Raccourci Agency became my first client as a freelancer. Around the same time, I took Bruno Simon's Three.js Journey (opens in new tab) course, which opened the door to WebGL and creative development. That's where most of my current energy goes: exploring what's possible when code meets visual expression.
