Since the *Ocarina of Time* remake was announced, I can't get those songs out of my head. So I made an ocarina you can play in your browser.

The ocarina floats on the stump in Link's house, fairies fly around the room, and the five N64 buttons play the original in-game samples. Play one of the 21 songs from *Ocarina of Time* and *Majora's Mask* and it answers with the "correct" jingle, then replays it in rhythm and adds it to the song book.
